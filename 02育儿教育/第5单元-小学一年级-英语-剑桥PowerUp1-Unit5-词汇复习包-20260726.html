④ 三、核心知识点详解（双步讲解）

1. Toys and colours

第一步·框架：名词说“是什么”，形容词说“什么样”。英语中颜色通常放在名词前：a blue kite；数量为一个时常用 a/an。

📌必背：a red car；a grey robot；a yellow toy house；a blue kite；a red and grey plane。
🧠拓展：看到图片先说颜色，再补物品；例如“orange + bike”→ an orange bike。

题型映射：看图配词、What colour is...?、短文信息提取。【核验来源：Power Up 1 2E+Cambridge English词汇资源】

2. Whose and possessives

第一步·框架：whose询问“谁的”；回答用人名+’s或物主代词：Whose car is this? It’s Jenny’s.

📌必背：Jim’s kite；Jenny’s car；Tom’s robot；his/her/their present。
适用边界：It’s Jim’s正确；It’s Jim回答的是“是谁”，不是“谁的”。
🧠拓展：名词所有格的 ’s 表示“属于”，不是复数；Jenny’s可单独作回答。

【核验来源：Power Up 1 2E+Cambridge Dictionary grammar】

3. Do/does questions and wants

第一步·框架：主语he/she/it用does，I/you/we/they用do；does出现后，动词回到原形。

📌句型：Does he want a teddy? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn’t. What does she want? — She wants a balloon.
易错：Does she wants×；应为Does she want。但肯定句是She wants。

【核验来源：Power Up 1 2E+Cambridge English grammar】

4. Shapes and robot

第一步·框架：用has got描述拥有，用is/are描述形状和颜色：Its head is a green circle.

📌必背：circle圆形；square正方形；triangle三角形；rectangle长方形；head头、body身体、eyes眼睛、mouth嘴、arms手臂、legs腿、feet脚。
🧠拓展：its是“它的”，it’s是it is；Its eyes are...因eyes为复数用are。

【核验来源：Power Up 1 2E+Cambridge English + Cambridge Dictionary】

5. Sharing story

第一步·框架：故事按“拥有—请求—冲突—道歉—分享—快乐”推进，理解代词指代和因果。

📌必背：Can I play with...? Here you are. I’m sorry. share toys. The twins are happy again.
🧠拓展：share带来合作，say sorry修复关系；复述时用First/Then/But/Finally。

【核验来源：Power Up 1 2E故事主题+Cambridge English Pre-A1 Starters speaking functions】

⑥ 五、考点汇总

  • 15个基础玩具词与颜色词，能听、认、读、拼。
  • Whose + 名词？— It’s + 人名’s.
  • his/her/their与名词搭配；Jim and Jenny’s birthday。
  • Do/Does一般疑问句；What does...want? — He/She wants...
  • 四种形状与机器人部件；its/it’s辨析。
  • 阅读五要素：人物、物品、颜色、动作、情绪；能用证据回答。

⑦ 六、薄弱环节专项突破

💡根据材料推断重点薄弱：所有格/物主代词、does后动词原形、阅读代词指代、形状词汇拼写。
  1. 三秒归属法：看到“谁的”圈whose；看到回答人名，在末尾加’s。
  2. Does开关：句首有Does，后面的动词不加s；答案短语保留does。
  3. 代词回指：把it/they/them替换回前一句最近且意义匹配的名词。
  4. 形状画读：画圆、正方、三角、长方，再给机器人贴标签。
点击打开：亲子口头操练

家长举玩具问：Whose...? 孩子答：It’s ...’s. 再问：What does ... want? 孩子答完整句。

⑧ 七、康奈尔笔记·知识加工厂

每张笔记都要求“遮住主栏→看线索栏回忆”；主栏是重构，不是抄写。

笔记卡1-2：词汇与所属关系
线索栏（提问）主栏（重构+例子）
哪些词能描述生日礼物？把物品和颜色打包记忆：a blue kite、a red car、a grey robot、a toy plane。看到生日场景，先定位人物，再找礼物。
怎样问“谁的”？Whose + 名词 + is this/that? 回答It’s Jenny’s. 例如看到红车：Whose car is that? It’s Jenny’s.

总结：颜色在前、物品在后；whose问归属，’s回答归属。

笔记卡3-5：语法、形状、故事
线索栏主栏（重构+例子）
Does之后怎样？Does she want a balloon? Yes, she does. 问句动词用原形，肯定陈述才用wants。
its和it’s如何分？Its head is a circle（它的）；It’s green（It is）。eyes复数：Its eyes are red.
故事如何复述？先拥有玩具，再请求分享；争吵后say sorry，最后share again and laugh。关键词：siblings, argue, happy。

总结：语法由主语决定；故事由因果链串起来。

笔记卡6-7：总览与单元核心句
线索栏主栏（重构+例子）
本单元四条线怎样连？用“玩具”连接颜色、所属、想要、形状和分享：Jenny’s red car；What does he want?；Its body is a square。
最值得背的句子？Whose toy is this? It’s Tom’s. / What does she want? She wants a doll. / Can I play with your bear? Here you are.

总结：Unit 5用玩具语境练习“描述—归属—需求—合作”。

⑩ 九、基础练习1

  1. Whose bike is this? It’s ______. (Jim / Jim’s)
  2. The kite is ______. (blue / blues)
  3. What colour is the robot? It’s ______. (grey / whose)
  4. Jenny has a red ______. (car / cars)
  5. 选择：A ______ has four equal sides. A circle B triangle C square
答案：1 Jim’s；2 blue；3 grey；4 car；5 C。自检：每题说出“线索词”。

⑪ 十、基础练习2

  1. ______ he want a teddy? A Do B Does
  2. What does she ______? A want B wants
  3. She ______ a balloon. A want B wants
  4. Its eyes ______ red. A is B are
  5. Can I play ______ your robot? A with B for
  6. 填空：This is ______ (Jenny) car.
答案：1 B；2 A；3 B；4 B；5 A；6 Jenny’s。

⑫ 十一、提高练习1

读句子回答：Tom has a grey robot. Jenny has a red car. Jim’s favourite present is a blue kite.

  1. Whose robot is grey?
  2. What colour is Jenny’s car?
  3. What is Jim’s favourite present?
  4. 改错：Does she wants a doll?
答案：1 Tom’s. 2 Red. 3 A blue kite. 4 Does she want a doll?

⑬ 十二、提高练习2

读：My robot has a green circle head, red circle eyes and a white rectangle mouth. Its body is a square.

  1. What shape is its head?
  2. What colour is its mouth?
  3. 用一句话描述身体。
  4. 辨析：Its body is green. / It’s body is green.
答案：1 A circle. 2 White. 3 Its body is a square. 4 第一句正确。

⑭ 十三、拓展练习

开放任务：设计一个“birthday toy robot”，画出至少4种形状，写4句介绍。

句型支架：My robot has got... / Its head is... / Its eyes are... / It’s my favourite present.
评分：形状词2分；颜色词2分；has got/its准确2分；句子完整2分；朗读清楚2分。

⑮ 十四、综合模拟卷（721结构）

  1. Whose plane is red and grey? A Jim’s B Grandma’s C Eva’s
  2. Does Ben want a board game? — No, he ______. (doesn’t/does)
  3. What does she want? — She ______ a doll. (want/wants)
  4. The robot’s body is a ______. (square/circle)
  5. 阅读：Lucy shares a spaceship with Ben. They argue, say sorry and share again. 主题是______。
  6. 写一句请求分享的话。
答案：1 B；2 doesn’t；3 wants；4 square；5 sharing and apologizing；6 Can I play with your spaceship, please?（示例）。

⑯ 十五、错题清零专卷

  3. Does he wants a kite? → __________________
  4. Its eyes is red. → __________________
  5. Jim kite is blue. → __________________
  6. She doesn’t likes dolls. → __________________
答案：1 Whose car is this? 2 It’s Jenny’s. 3 Does he want a kite? 4 Its eyes are red. 5 Jim’s kite is blue. 6 She doesn’t like dolls.
