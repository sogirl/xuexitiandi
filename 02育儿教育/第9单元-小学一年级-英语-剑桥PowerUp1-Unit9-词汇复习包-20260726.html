  • be 动词主谓一致：I am / He-She-It is / You-We-They are

  • 用 this/that/these 替代衣服 + 单复数呼应（This T-shirt / These jeans）

四、核心知识点详解（双步讲解法）

本章按双步讲解法对 Unit 9 五大核心知识点进行「原理推导 + 必背拓展 + 题型映射」。

知识点 1 · 现在进行时（be + V-ing）：介绍家人正在穿的衣服

🔍 第一步 · 原理推导
现在进行时表达「此时此刻正在发生的动作」，结构 = be 动词（am/is/are）+ V-ing。本单元聚焦「现在正在穿什么」，因此常用 be wearing 结构。主语决定 be 动词：I → am / He-She-It → is / You-We-They → are。当 Grandmother 现在正穿一件粉裙子，说 "Grandma is wearing a pink skirt."，强调「此刻·正在·穿」。
📌 第二步 · 必背句型
  • 肯定：He is wearing blue jeans.
  • 否定：She isn't wearing a hat now.
  • 一般疑问：Is Grandpa wearing green boots? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
  • 特殊疑问：What is Jenny wearing? — A blue skirt.
🧠 拓展 · wear / is wearing / puts on 三者辨析
  • wear = 「穿着·戴着」（状态延续）：She wears glasses every day.
  • is wearing = 「现在正穿着」（瞬间状态）：She is wearing a pink dress today.
  • puts on / is putting on = 「正在穿上」（动作进行）：She is putting on her shoes now.
📋 题型映射：Pu1 Unit9 题 1（is wearing）、题 7（is wearing now）、题 12（doesn't like）→ 全部命中「现在进行时 be + V-ing」语法点。

知识点 2 · 助动词 Do / Does：询问喜好与穿着

🔍 第一步 · 原理推导
当想用一般疑问句询问「某人是否喜欢某物」，需要在句首加助动词。主语决定助动词：do 用于 you / we / they 及复数名词；does 用于 he / she / it 及单数第三人称。注意：用了助动词 do/does 后，实义动词要回原形（不加 s）。
📌 第二步 · 必背句型
  • Do you like the pink dress? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Does Grandma like the yellow T-shirt? — Yes, she does.
  • Does Jenny like pink? — No, she doesn't like pink.
  • Are you wearing a hat? — Yes, I am.
  • Is he wearing boots? — Yes, he is.
⚠️ 易错点：Does he likes ❌ → Does he like ✓；He don't like ❌ → He doesn't like ✓；Does he like 与 Is he wearing 不可混用——前者问喜好（一般现在时），后者问正穿的衣服（现在进行时）。
🧠 拓展 · 否定缩略与完整形：don't = do not · doesn't = does not · isn't = is not · aren't = are not。缩写常用于口语与书面，完整形强调语气。
📋 题型映射：Pu1 题 2（Does Jenny like）、题 4（Do you want）、题 11（Does Grandma like）、题 12（doesn't like）→ 全部命中助动词 do/does 语法点。

知识点 3 · 祈使句（动词原形开头）：下达指令

🔍 第一步 · 原理推导
祈使句表达命令、请求、叮嘱，主语常被省略（默认是 you），句首直接用动词原形。否定式在动词原形前加 Don't。本单元高频动词：Clean / Pick up / Put / Look at / Point to / Open / Don't eat / Don't jump / Let's play。
📌 第二步 · 必背句型
  • Clean the dirty table, please!（打扫脏桌子！）
  • Pick up the hat!（捡起帽子！）
  • Put it on the table.（把它放在桌子上。）
  • Look at the barn!（看那个谷仓！）
  • Don't eat oranges on the floor!（不要在地板上吃橙子！）
  • Let's go and play in the barn!（我们去谷仓玩吧！）
⚠️ 易错点：1) 代词放中间：Pick up it ❌ → Pick it up ✓（动词短语 + 代词 → 代词放中间）；2) 否定用 Don't + 动词原形：Don't eating ❌ → Don't eat ✓；3) Look at 是介词短语动词，at 不能丢；4) Let's = Let us，后必接动词原形：Let's to play ❌ → Let's play ✓。
🧠 拓展 · 祈使句 + 名词 vs 代词
  • Pick up the hat ✓（名词短语可放动词后任意位置）
  • Pick it up ✓（代词必须放中间）
  • Take those pencils and put them in the bag.（them 是代词→放 put 中间）
📋 题型映射：Pu1 Story+Grammar 改错题（共 10 题）全部命中祈使句动词原形、否定、代词位置。

知识点 4 · 介词 in / on / at / next to：描述物品位置

🔍 第一步 · 原理推导
英语介词表达「位置与方向」与中文思维不同，本单元核心三介词：
  • in = 在…里面（如水里、太阳里、海里）：in the sea / in the sun
  • on = 在…上面（接触面）：on the sand / on the beach / on a boat
  • at = 在某地点：at the beach
  • next to = 紧邻：next to the sea / next to a beach
📌 第二步 · 必背短语
  • at the beach 在海滩（地点）
  • on the sand 在沙滩上（接触面）
  • in the sun 在阳光下（包含关系）
  • next to the sea 紧邻大海（相邻）
  • in a boat on the sea 在海上的小船里（双重定位）
  • under the umbrella 在伞下（隐藏）
⚠️ 易错点：at the beach（表示「在那个海滩·地点」），on the beach（表示「在沙滩表面·接触面」）—— 同为 beach 含义不同！短语 in the sun 「在阳光下」用 in 而非 under。
🧠 拓展 · at / on / in 与交通工具：
  • in a car / in a boat（人在车/船内部）
  • on a bike / on a horse（人骑在表面）
  • at the airport / at the beach（具体地点）
📋 题型映射：Vocabulary+Grammar Ⅱ 题 1/9/10/16、Cross-Curricular 题 5 → 介词 in/on/at next to 辨析。

知识点 5 · like / enjoy + V-ing：表达兴趣爱好

🔍 第一步 · 原理推导
表达「喜欢做某事」，用 like / enjoy + V-ing 形式（动名词作宾语）。注意：主语是第三人称单数时，like 要加 s（likes），enjoy 不变。回答「我也喜欢」用 So do I（助动词 do 提前）或 Me too（简略口语）。
📌 第二步 · 必背句型
  • I like swimming in the sea. 我喜欢在海里游泳。
  • I like flying my kite. 我喜欢放风筝。
  • I enjoy taking photos. 我享受拍照片。
  • I enjoy fishing. 我享受钓鱼。
  • — I enjoy taking photos. — Me too.（"我也一样"·口语）
  • — I like flying my kite. — So do I.（"我也（喜欢）"·正式）
⚠️ 易错点：1) like to do ❌ → like doing ✓；2) like doing 不可拆；3) He like ❌ → He likes ✓；4) enjoy to do ❌ → enjoy doing ✓；5) I am liking ❌（like 是状态动词，不用于进行时）。
🧠 拓展 · So do I / Me too / So I do 区别
  • So do I. = I also do it. （肯定·强调动作）— I like swimming. — So do I.
  • Me too. = I am the same. （口语·简略）— I enjoy fishing. — Me too.
  • So I do. = I certainly do. （强调主语确实如此）— You look tired. — So I do.
  • I too. ❌（不符合英语习惯）
📋 题型映射：Vocabulary+Grammar Ⅱ 题 5/11/12/17、Cross-Curricular 题 1-10、Literature 题 6-10 → 全部命中 like/enjoy + V-ing。

五、难点与易错点

🔴 难点 1 · 衣物复数：trousers / shorts / jeans 永远复数

英语中trousers / shorts / jeans / glasses / sunglasses 这类「由两片构成的衣物」只有复数形式，搭配 are / these / those，不能说 a trouser。谓语必须用复数：The orange shorts are ugly.

易错写法 ❌正确写法 ✓
The short is ugly.The shorts are ugly.
This jeans is nice.These jeans are nice.
The sunglass is on the table.The sunglasses are on the table.
This trouser is big.These trousers are big.

🔴 难点 2 · 祈使句动词原形 vs -s/-ing

祈使句必须以动词原形开头，不能加 s，也不能加 ing。否定式 Don't + 动词原形，不能写 Don't eating。

易错 ❌正确 ✓
Puts the hat on the table.Put the hat on the table.
Cleaning your face, please!Clean your face, please!
Looked at the barn!Look at the barn!
Not eat the oranges!Don't eat the oranges!
Let's to play in the barn!Let's play in the barn!

🔴 难点 3 · 代词位置：Pick up it ❌

含「动词 + 介词」的短语动词（如 pick up / put on / clean up）接代词时，代词必须放中间，不能放短语末尾。接名词则两端都可以。

易错 ❌正确 ✓
Pick up it!Pick it up!
Point to she!Point to her!
Look at they!Look at them!
Clean it up now.Clean up it.（错）✓ Clean it up.

🔴 难点 4 · this/that/these/those 与衣物呼应

指代单数衣物用 this / that，复数衣物（trousers/shorts/jeans）用 these / those。谓语动词也必须呼应单复数。

易错 ❌正确 ✓
This jeans are small for you.These jeans are small for you.
This T-shirt is my favourite.This T-shirt is my favourite.（单数✓）
How are my shoes? — It is very orange.— They are very orange.

🔴 难点 5 · like 用法：第三人称单数 likes

主语是 he/she/it 时，like → likes，否定 doesn't like，疑问 Does she like…?

易错 ❌正确 ✓
She like pink.She likes pink.
He don't like the hat.He doesn't like the hat.
Does she likes it?Does she like it?
Jenny don't like pink.Jenny doesn't like pink.

🔴 难点 6 · enjoy + V-ing：不能接 to do

enjoy 只能接 V-ing，不能接 to do；主语是任何人都用 enjoy，不加 s。

易错 ❌正确 ✓
I enjoy to take photos.I enjoy taking photos.
She enjoys playing.She enjoys playing.（不变 s 是 enjoys 名词→动词三单）
They enjoy to walk in the forest.They enjoy walking in the forest.

七、薄弱环节专项突破

基于 Pu1 Unit9 拓展练习的题目分布与孩子常见错点，本章聚焦最易失分的 5 个薄弱环节进行专项训练，每环节 4-6 题。

⚡ 薄弱 1 · 祈使句代词位置（最高频失分）

📝 祈使句代词位置专项（6 题）
  1. Pick up the hat. — Pick ______ up!（代词放中间）
    答案：it · 解析：代词必须放动词短语中间。
  2. Point to Shelly. — Point to ______!（不可用 she）
    答案：her · 解析：人称代词宾格：she → her。
  3. Look at the pencils. — Look at ______!（复数代词）
    答案：them · 解析：复数宾格 they → them。
  4. Clean the table! — Clean ______ up now!（代词放中间）
    答案：it · 解析：代词 it 放在 clean up 中间。
  5. Pick up the clothes! — Pick ______ up, Shelly!
    答案：them · 解析：复数 clothes → them。
  6. Put the bag on the table! — Put ______ on the table!
    答案：it · 解析：单数 bag → it。

⚡ 薄弱 2 · 助动词 Do/Does 选错

📝 助动词专项（6 题）
  1. ______ Jenny like the pink dress?
    答案：Does · 解析：Jenny 是第三人称单数 → Does。
  2. Grandma asks: "______ you want them?"
    答案：Do · 解析：you → Do。
  3. ______ Grandma like the yellow T-shirt?
    答案：Does · 解析：Grandma 单数 → Does。
  4. ______ you want a new skirt?
    答案：Do · 解析：you → Do。
  5. Jenny ______ pink because it isn't her favourite colour.
    答案：doesn't like · 解析：第三人称单数否定 → doesn't + 原形 like。
  6. He ______ like the orange shoes. They are ugly.
    答案：doesn't · 解析：He → doesn't。

⚡ 薄弱 3 · like/enjoy + V-ing 用错

📝 like/enjoy doing 专项（6 题）
  1. I ______ flying kites at the beach.
    答案：like · 解析：I am 主语 → like + V-ing。
  2. My sister ______ taking photos of flowers.
    答案：enjoys · 解析：第三人称单数 enjoys + V-ing。
  3. We ______ climbing mountains on holiday.
    答案：like · 解析：we → like + V-ing。
  4. They enjoy ______ in the forest.
    答案：walking · 解析：enjoy + 动名词 walking，不能 to walk。
  5. I like ______ frogs by the river.
    答案：seeing · 解析：like + 动名词 seeing。
  6. Children ______ learning animal stories.
    答案：like · 解析：children 复数 → like + V-ing。

⚡ 薄弱 4 · 介词 in/on/at/next to 辨析

📝 介词专项（6 题）
  1. We are ______ the beach now.
    答案：at · 解析：at the beach = 在海滩（地点）。
  2. They are playing ______ the sun at the beach.
    答案：in · 解析：in the sun = 在阳光下（包含关系）。
  3. The umbrella is ______ the beach chair.
    答案：next to · 解析：next to = 紧邻。
  4. Let's go ______ in the sea.
    答案：fishing · 解析：go fishing = 去钓鱼（固定搭配）。
  5. My sister is collecting ______ on the beach.
    答案：shells · 解析：collect shells（贝壳复数）。
  6. The water in the ______ is very blue.
    答案：sea · 解析：in the sea = 在大海里。

⚡ 薄弱 5 · So do I / Me too 选择

📝 回应专项（4 题）
  1. — I like flying my kite. — ______.
    答案：So do I · 解析：动词 like → So + do + 主语。
  2. — I enjoy taking photos. — ______.
    答案：Me too · 解析：enjoy doing 后用 Me too 简略回答。
  3. — I enjoy fishing. — ______.
    答案：Me too · 解析：享受类用 Me too 更自然。
  4. — Do you like playing at the beach? — ______.
    答案：Yes, I do · 解析：Do 疑问 → Yes, I do / No, I don't。

📌 笔记 2/7 · 核心语法·现在进行时+一般现在时双轨

🎯 双轨语法核心

🔑 线索栏

  • be 动词如何选？
  • 何时用 is wearing？
  • likes 加 s 何时？
  • 句首加 Do 还是 Does？
  • So do I 用在哪里？

📝 主栏 · 笔记

轨道 A · 现在进行时 be + V-ing（描述「此刻正在」）

公式：主语 + be + V-ing。主谓一致：I am / He-She-It is / You-We-They are。例：He is wearing blue jeans now.

轨道 B · 一般现在时 like + V-ing（表达「一贯喜好」）

公式：主语 + like/enjoy + V-ing。注意第三人称单数加 s：She likes pink.，否定 doesn't like，疑问 Does she like…?

回应公式：肯定用 So + 助动词 + 主语 或 Me too。例：— I like swimming. — So do I. / Me too.

辨别口诀：有 now / Look! / Listen! → 现在进行时；有 always / usually / every day → 一般现在时。

💡 总结区

本单元两条语法主线：现在进行时（正在穿）+ 一般现在时（喜欢做什么）。孩子要能从语境信号词（now vs always）准确切换时态。

📌 笔记 3/7 · 三大难点对照清单

🎯 易错点速记卡

🔑 线索栏

  • shorts 谓语用 is 还是 are？
  • Puts 还是 Put？
  • Pick up it 错在哪？
  • like to do 还是 like doing？
  • I too 对吗？

📝 主栏 · 易错点 1-3

1. 衣物复数（短裙裤眼镜总是复数）：trousers / shorts / jeans / sunglasses / glasses 永远复数，谓语用 are。例：The orange shorts are ugly.

2. 祈使句动词原形（绝不能加 s/ing/ed）：Put（不是 Puts/Putting/Putted）；Don't eat（不是 Don't eating）。

3. 代词位置（动词短语+代词 → 代词放中间）：Pick up it ❌ → Pick it up ✓；Point to she ❌ → Point to her ✓。

📝 主栏 · 易错点 4-6

4. like/enjoy + 动名词（不能 to do）：I like swimming ✓；I enjoy fishing ✓；He likes playing ✓。

5. So do I / Me too 区分：动词类 → So do I；状态/情绪 → Me too。I too ❌ 不规范。

6. this/that/these/those 与单复数：These jeans are small（复数+are）；This T-shirt is big（单数+is）。

💡 总结区

三大难点都指向「形式与语义匹配」：复数衣物 → 复数谓语；代词 → 短语中间；动名词 → enjoy/like 后。每日复习时让孩子口头翻译 3 个错例是最快巩固法。

十、基础练习 1（衣物词汇·Vocabulary Ⅰ）

难度：⭐⭐ · 共 12 题 · 考查衣物名词识别与主谓一致。

第 1 部分 · 单选题（8 题）

  1. The ______ are ugly. (A. shorts B. boots C. skirt D. shoes)
    答案：A · shorts 是复数名词，谓语 are；裙子/靴子/鞋均为单数搭配 the 即可但需视空后谓语。
  2. The ______ are very nice! (A. sunglasses B. boots C. trousers D. shoes 单数)
    答案：A · sunglasses 为复数，谓语 are。
  3. Grandpa ______ green boots now. (A. wear B. wears C. is wearing D. are wearing)
    答案：C · now 时间标志 → 现在进行时 is wearing。
  4. Jenny ______ a blue skirt now. (A. wears B. is wearing C. wear D. are wearing)
    答案：B · Jenny 第三人称单数 + now → is wearing。
  5. Grandpa's shirt is very ______. (A. ugly B. big C. colourful D. small)
    答案：C · colourful「色彩鲜艳的」直接对应文意。
  6. How are my shoes? — ______ are very orange. (A. It B. He C. She D. They)
    答案：D · shoes 复数 → They。
  7. "______ jeans are small for you." (A. This B. That C. These D. They)
    答案：C · jeans 复数 → These。
  8. My blue ______ is on the chair. (A. shirts B. trousers C. shoes 单数)
    答案：B · trousers 是复数，但 is 用于作主语的整个衣物短语。

第 2 部分 · 翻译题（4 题）

  1. 中译英：她今天戴着一顶黄帽子。
    She is wearing a yellow hat today.
  2. 中译英：这件 T 恤太大了。
    This T-shirt is too big.
  3. 中译英：粉色棒球帽。
    The pink baseball cap.
  4. 中译英：橙色的短裤很丑。
    The orange shorts are ugly.
✅ 基础练习 1 · 答案速查
  1. A · 2. A · 3. C · 4. B · 5. C · 6. D · 7. C · 8. B · 9. She is wearing a yellow hat today. · 10. This T-shirt is too big. · 11. The pink baseball cap. · 12. The orange shorts are ugly.

十一、基础练习 2（海滩词汇·Vocabulary Ⅱ）

难度：⭐⭐ · 共 12 题 · 考查海滩名词 + 介词 in/on/at/next to。

第 1 部分 · 单选题（10 题）

  1. We are ______ the beach now. (A. in B. on C. at D. under)
    答案：C · at the beach = 在海滩（地点）。
  2. The big ______ is blue and beautiful. (A. beach B. sea C. sand D. sun)
    答案：B · sea 是蓝色的。
  3. The ______ is shining brightly. (A. beach B. sea C. sand D. sun)
